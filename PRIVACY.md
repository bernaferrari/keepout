# Keepout privacy policy

Keepout is an Android firewall, DNS tool, and VPN app maintained by Bernardo Ferrari. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email [bernaferrari2@gmail.com](mailto:bernaferrari2@gmail.com).

## Traffic and activity

Keepout uses Android's VPN service to apply firewall and DNS rules. It uses the installed-app list and network metadata such as app identifiers, domain names, IP addresses, ports, timestamps, and connection outcomes to apply rules and show activity. Network and DNS activity logs are stored on your device. You can turn off logging, choose a retention period, and clear logged activity. Manual exports and backups go to the location or app you select. Android may back up eligible settings according to your device settings. Keepout excludes its traffic-log database from Android backup.

Your DNS resolver receives the queries you send to it. If you configure a remote VPN or proxy, that service receives the traffic routed to it. The protocols and services you choose determine who can see that traffic and how it is protected in transit. The optional Rethink Proxy Network service is operated by Rethink DNS. See the [Rethink DNS privacy policy](https://www.rethinkdns.com/privacy) for its handling of data.

Keepout can use Android Accessibility to identify the foreground app when you enable the firewall rule that blocks background apps. Keepout uses that information on the device to apply the rule. Optional Wi-Fi automation uses the current network name (SSID) to decide when a tunnel should run. Android requires location permission to provide that name, including in the background when the automation runs.

## Diagnostics and subscriptions

Google Play's Android vitals can report crashes and app freezes through Android's usage and diagnostics setting. Keepout stores its own diagnostic files locally. If you choose to send a bug report, Keepout prepares the files for you to share through an app you choose.

If you buy a subscription through Google Play, Google handles the payment. Subscription and device identifiers, purchase tokens, and entitlement information may be sent to the subscription service to verify the purchase, connect it to your device, and manage access. Contact support for questions about subscription records or retention. Keepout contains no ads.

## Your choices

You can clear traffic logs in the app, remove local settings and records by clearing app storage, and delete any exports or backups you saved. For information already sent to Keepout support or its service providers, contact the address above. Android’s usage and diagnostics setting controls participation in Android vitals.
